# Global Music Streaming Trends & Listener Insights
An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project investigating how consumer metrics—such as age, subscription model, and platform choice—impact global streaming behavior. 
## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Language:** Python 3
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Environment:** Anaconda / Jupyter Notebook
## 📈 Key Insights Discovered
1. **The Streaming Giants Market Share:** The streaming landscape is intensely fragmented. Amazon Music leads marginally at 17.2%, with Tidal (17%) and Deezer (16.9%) closely trailing, proving no single app dominates consumer attention.
2. **The Genre Shift:** Contrary to expectations, traditional Pop music ranked lowest in total listeners, while Reggae, Jazz, and EDM emerged as the top streaming genres globally. 
3. **Age Correlation:** A scatter plot analysis of Age vs. Daily Streaming Minutes revealed a flat distribution (zero correlation). Listening volume is uniform across generations; age does not dictate engagement duration.
4. **Premium Conversion Strategy:** YouTube shows the highest internal conversion rate from Free to Premium tiers, while Amazon Music retains a massive unmonetized Free tier user base.

## 🚀 Strategic Business Recommendations
- **For Amazon Music:** Launch targeted promotional campaign discounts to convert their heavily populated "Free" tier block into paid subscribers.
- **For Marketing Teams:** Shift global ad-spend allocations toward Jazz and Reggae curated playlists rather than traditional Pop to capture the highest density of active listeners.
